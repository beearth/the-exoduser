## English short description

EXODUSER: HELL LORD is a dark fantasy action RPG set in Hell, combining evasive movement and parries with loot and skill combinations. The demo is being prepared around the Greatsword Warrior, with a focus on how combat, equipment choices, and skill fusion work together.

## English body

Combat in Hell begins with taking the fight to its enemies. Watch their movements, close the distance to strike, and pull away when danger approaches. EXODUSER: HELL LORD combines movement and defensive action with hack-and-slash battles against groups of enemies.

Evade to move out of an attack’s path, and parry to meet an enemy’s assault. Deciding when to approach and when to retreat is part of the fight. Weave movement and defense between your attacks to find a combat rhythm that suits you.

The equipment you find in combat leads to your next decision. Consider its abilities alongside your build, and combine skills to try a different approach to attacking. Skill fusion connects learned abilities into new combinations, giving you choices about which equipment and skills to use together.

The demo is being prepared as an introduction to this loop, following the Greatsword Warrior through the opening area. Its focus is on responding to enemy attacks, comparing equipment, and exploring skill combinations to understand how action and progression connect. It introduces EXODUSER’s direction to players who enjoy making decisions both in combat and in their builds.
