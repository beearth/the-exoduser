## One-liner

Parry bullet storms, battle demon hordes, and forge powerful builds in a dark fantasy action RPG.

## Short description

A story-focused dark fantasy action RPG built around loot, skill combinations, and active combat. Battle swarms of enemies, experiment with your build, and turn incoming projectiles against them with well-timed parries.

## Description

EXODUSER: HELL LORD combines the scale and build variety of hack-and-slash games with combat driven by player decisions. Movement, positioning, and the timing of a parry should matter even when the screen is full of enemies.

Large-scale battles meet parryable projectile patterns. Players actively read incoming attacks, reposition, and time their defense while fighting enemy hordes. Equipment, active skills, and skill fusion support different builds without removing the importance of player control. The dark fantasy setting gives this struggle a clear identity: surviving an overwhelming world through deliberate action.

*THOSE WHO DEFY THE FALL. EXODUSER.*

## Key features — current build

- **Real-time hack-and-slash** against large enemy hordes.
- **Projectile parrying** — read bullet patterns by their color and shape, then time your defense to answer the pressure.
- **High-speed chain traversal** for fast repositioning in crowded fights.
- **Active skills and skill fusion** — combine skills into new forms.
- **Equipment farming and character builds** — item and equipment combinations shape how you fight.
- **Ancestral spirit summons** as an additional combat option.

The free demo focuses on the **Greatsword Warrior**.

## In development — not in the demo

These are development targets and are **not** presented as playable content:

- Four additional character concepts: **Hell Hunter** (gothic revolver-rifle), **Transmuter** (core gauntlets forming pure-energy weapons such as a scythe and bow), **Arcane Lancer** (lance-staff combining spear combat and arcane spellcasting), and **Silvertail / Blade Dancer** (mobile melee with a large back-mounted rotating blade).
- A full-game design targeting a seven-chapter journey across 35 areas, with more enemies, bosses, build options, and repeatable dungeon challenges.

## About the developer

FOR DEAR GAMERS (FDG) is a full-time solo developer from South Korea. EXODUSER: HELL LORD is the studio's first release. Development priorities are combat readability, responsive controls, distinct weapon identities, and reliable saved progression.
