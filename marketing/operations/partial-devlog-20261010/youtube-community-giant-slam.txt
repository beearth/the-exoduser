Skill notes | Hell Slam I
Hell Slam I strikes nearby enemies with a circular shockwave. Stored rage increases its damage and is consumed when used.
It can also be used without rage if its other activation requirements are met. Strike now or build more rage for a stronger hit—which approach suits your playstyle?
