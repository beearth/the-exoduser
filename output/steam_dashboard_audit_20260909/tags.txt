  - heading "About This Game" [level=2]
  - paragraph: "EXODUSER: HELL LORD is a dark fantasy hack-and-slash ARPG about climbing out of the seven layers of hell."
  - paragraph: "The moment a brutal revenge ended in its final execution, a blade pierced him from behind. He woke at the bottom of hell. He does not remember how he fell, but one thing is certain: his children are alive above the surface, waiting. Seven layers of damnation and thirty-five stages stand between him and them."
  - paragraph: The goddess of vengeance Nemesia walks at his side, claiming to guide him. Whether her help is real or another trap, no one can say. Pick up your blade and survive for now. Survive long enough, and even hell ends somewhere.
  - paragraph: Wield a colossal greatsword to carve through hundreds of demons in real time. Master Bullet Language, a color-keyed parry system that turns enemy projectiles back into weapons. Use Chain Drive grappling to close gaps or escape danger. A black cat named Diroy, a raven called Hector, and a ghost-bound crossbow accompany the climb.
  - paragraph: KEY FEATURES
  - paragraph: Real-time mass combat. No attack-ticket systems or artificial slowdowns.
  - paragraph: Bullet Language. A color-keyed parry system for deflecting deadly bullet patterns.
  - paragraph: Chain Drive. Hold-charge chain mobility for closing or breaking distance.
  - paragraph: Seven chapters, thirty-five-plus stages, thirty-five-plus bosses. Each carries their own tragedy.
  - paragraph: Skill fusion. Six archetypes, five rarity tiers, and over twenty-one fusion combinations.
  - paragraph: Live service. The door to player feedback stays open, and the fusion and build space keep growing.
  - paragraph: A love letter to dark fantasy, hand-crafted by a solo developer at FDG (FOR DEAR GAMERS).
