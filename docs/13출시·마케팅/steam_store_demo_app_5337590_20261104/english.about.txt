[p]The demo lets you play through 1-1, the first region of hell, from start to finish. Begin as a level 1 Warrior, get hands-on with the greatsword, starting skills, bullet parrying, Chain Drive and loot, then face the region's first boss, the Dark Druid. The demo level cap is 100, and demo progress is saved separately from the full game. Skill fusion, more heroes, and the deeper layers of hell and their bosses await in the full game.[/p]
