The Supported Languages section now lists Korean for interface and subtitles only. Other in-game language choices remain incomplete and are not claimed as supported. No full audio language is claimed.

To check the Korean interface, launch the game and choose Korean from the language selector on the title/character-selection screen. During gameplay, press Esc and choose Settings > Language; the selection applies immediately.

To check the Korean tutorial instructions, create or select a character and enter the first playable stage. Follow the on-screen movement and attack tutorial prompts.

To check the Korean subtitles in the character-creation story video, start creating a new character and watch the story video that plays during character creation. The Korean subtitle text is embedded in the video.

“Captions available” has been removed because the game does not provide descriptive captions for non-speech sounds. This accessibility feature is separate from spoken-dialogue subtitles.

We are not claiming that all selectable language translations are complete or that the controller feedback has been resolved.
